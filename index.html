Videography Short Film

Befolgte Schritte

Director / Editor / Lead Actress

My first university short film, created for my GER101 final project, fully utilizing strictly the vocabulary taught in class.

❮ ❯

Project Overview

This film served as the final project for my GER101 class during my first term of my first year at the University of Waterloo. The creative constraint was to build a compelling story while strictly adhering to the basic vocabulary learned during the introductory term. This project marked my debut into university filmmaking and set a high standard for my subsequent creative works.

My Role

I served as the Director and Editor, while also performing as a Lead Actress. Managing these responsibilities simultaneously allowed me to oversee the artistic vision from production through to the final cut.

  • Post-Production: Completed the full edit in Adobe Premiere Pro, ensuring smooth pacing and narrative clarity.
  • Technical Management: Utilized professional production equipment, including a high-end camera and boom mic, to achieve a cinematic look and sound.
  • Collaborative Leadership: Led a dedicated production crew and coordinated with my actresses to bring the script to life.

Process & Thinking

The foundation of this project was true teamwork. I drafted the script collaboratively with my amazing GER101 classmates, ensuring that every line fit our vocabulary constraints while remaining impactful. To support our technical success on shoot day, I developed a detailed shot type list for the camera operator, which allowed for efficient filming and clear communication within our production team.

Outcome

The experience was both rewarding and transformative, teaching me the value of intense collaboration and technical preparation. The film was highly successful, earning my first Golden Boar Award at the University of Waterloo during my very first term of study.
